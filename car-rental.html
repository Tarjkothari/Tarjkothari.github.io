About the Project

This academic project focused on developing a comprehensive Car Rental Portal. The primary objective was to create a platform where users can easily browse and rent various cars at a low price determined by the website.

Key Responsibilities & Features
  • Team Leadership: Guided the team through the development lifecycle over a two-month period.
  • Full-Stack Development: Utilized PHP for backend operations and HTML/CSS/JS for creating an interactive frontend.
  • Booking Management: Designed the core logic to handle car availability and pricing structures.
